== Description ==

### Email Tracker - Professional Email Tracking & Analytics for WordPress Emails

**Email Tracker** is the most comprehensive email tracking solution for WordPress that monitors every email sent from your website. Track email opens, email link clicks, and gain valuable email insights to improve your email communication effectiveness.

#### Why Email Tracking Matters for Your WordPress Site

Every email your WordPress site sends is important - whether it's WooCommerce order confirmations, contact form notifications, password reset emails, or newsletter campaigns. Email Tracker ensures you know exactly when your emails are opened and which email links are clicked, giving you complete visibility into your email performance.

#### Complete Email Analytics & Monitoring

Email Tracker automatically tracks all WordPress emails sent through the native `wp_mail()` function, providing:

* **Real-time email open tracking** - Know instantly when your emails are read
* **Email link click tracking** - Monitor which links in your emails get clicked
* **Detailed email logs** - Complete history of all emails sent from your site
* **Email statistics** - View email open rates, click rates, and engagement metrics
* **IP tracking for emails** - See where your emails are being opened from
* **Email delivery confirmation** - Ensure your important emails reach their destination

This comprehensive email tracking helps you measure and improve your email marketing effectiveness, customer engagement, and overall email communication strategy.

== Installation ==

= Quick Email Tracker Installation =

1. Install Email Tracker through the WordPress plugins screen directly, or upload the plugin files to `/wp-content/plugins/email-tracker`
2. Activate Email Tracker through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress
3. That's it! Email Tracker will automatically start tracking all emails sent from your WordPress site
4. View your email tracking data by going to Email Tracker in your WordPress admin menu

= Start Tracking Your WordPress Emails =

* After activation, every email sent from WordPress will be tracked automatically
* Access email analytics from Email Tracker > Email List in your admin dashboard
* Send tracked emails manually from Email Tracker > Compose Email
* Configure email tracking settings from Email Tracker > Settings
* No additional configuration needed - email tracking starts immediately upon activation

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How many emails can I track using Email Tracker Plugin? =
You can track unlimited emails. Email Tracker monitors every email sent from your WordPress site without any restrictions on the number of emails tracked.

= Does Email Tracker work with all WordPress emails? =
Yes! Email Tracker automatically tracks all emails sent through WordPress's wp_mail() function, including WooCommerce emails, contact form emails, password reset emails, admin notification emails, and any other emails sent by WordPress or its plugins.

= Can I track when someone opens my email? =
Absolutely! Email Tracker provides real-time email open tracking. You'll see exactly when each email is opened, including the date, time, IP address, and how many times the email was viewed.

= Does Email Tracker require any third-party email service? =
No, Email Tracker works entirely within your WordPress installation. It doesn't require any external email services, APIs, or third-party email tracking services.

= How does email open tracking work? =
Email Tracker adds an invisible tracking pixel to your emails. When the recipient opens the email, the tracking pixel loads and Email Tracker records the email open event with all relevant details.

= Can I track links clicked in my emails? =
Yes! The Pro version of Email Tracker includes email link click tracking. You can see which links in your emails are clicked, when they're clicked, and by whom.

= Will Email Tracker slow down my email sending? =
No, Email Tracker is optimized for performance. Email tracking happens seamlessly in the background without affecting your email delivery speed.

= Can I see email statistics and analytics? =
Yes, Email Tracker provides comprehensive email analytics including open rates, click rates (Pro version), email history, and detailed logs for every email sent from your site.

= Does Email Tracker work with email marketing plugins? =
Email Tracker works with any plugin that uses WordPress's native wp_mail() function to send emails. This includes most contact form plugins, membership plugins, and e-commerce plugins.

= Is email tracking GDPR compliant? =
Email tracking itself is a technical feature. GDPR compliance depends on how you use it. We recommend informing your email recipients about email tracking in your privacy policy.

= What's the difference between Free and Pro versions? =
The Free version includes email open tracking, email logs, and basic email analytics. The Pro version adds email link click tracking, advanced email analytics, and priority support.
